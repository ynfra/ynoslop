**Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole (KVT Královo Pole)**

Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole (KVT Královo Pole) was an informal computing club in Brno, Czechoslovakia, based in the Královo Pole district. It was founded in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house and existed until 1983.

**History**

KVT Královo Pole was established in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house in Brno (Královo Pole district). The club’s membership was roughly a dozen for most of its life and reached about 40 at its peak in 1979. The club was dissolved in 1983 when the culture house withdrew the room used by the section.

**Notable project**

The club’s most notable project was "Kalkul 3" (1978), an 8-bit machine built from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. Three Kalkul 3 units were built; they were used by the city transport company to check tram timetable schedules.

**Archive**

Club papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 board are held by the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno, catalogued under fond KM-14.

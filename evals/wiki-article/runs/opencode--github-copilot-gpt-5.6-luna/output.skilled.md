## Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole

Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole (KVT Královo Pole) was founded in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house in the Královo Pole district of Brno, Czechoslovakia. The club had about 40 members at its peak in 1979 and roughly a dozen members for most of its existence.

In 1978, the club built three units of the 8-bit Kalkul 3 machine from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. The city transport company used the machines to check tram timetable schedules.

KVT Královo Pole was dissolved in 1983 after the culture house withdrew its room. The club's papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 board are held by the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno in fond KM-14.

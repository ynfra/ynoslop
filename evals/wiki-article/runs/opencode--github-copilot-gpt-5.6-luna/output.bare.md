## Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole

**Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole** (also known as **KVT Královo Pole**) was a hobby section of a local ROH culture house in Královo Pole, Brno, Czechoslovakia. It was founded in 1974.

The club had about 40 members at its peak in 1979 and roughly a dozen members for most of its existence. Its notable project was **Kalkul 3**, an 8-bit machine built in 1978 from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. Three units were constructed, and the machine was used by the city transport company to check tram timetable schedules.

KVT Královo Pole was dissolved in 1983 after the culture house withdrew its room. The club’s papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 board are held by the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno in fond KM-14.

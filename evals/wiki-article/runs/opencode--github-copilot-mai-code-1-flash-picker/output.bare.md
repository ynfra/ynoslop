Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole (KVT Královo Pole) was a computer club based in Brno, in the Královo Pole district of Czechoslovakia. It was founded in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house. At its peak in 1979 it had about 40 members, but for most of its life it numbered roughly a dozen.

The club’s best-known project was Kalkul 3 (1978), an 8-bit machine built from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. It was used by the city transport company to check tram timetable schedules, and three units were built. The club was dissolved in 1983 when the culture house withdrew the room. The club’s papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 board are held by the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno, in fond KM-14.

Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole (KVT Královo Pole) was founded in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house in Brno, in the Královo Pole district of Czechoslovakia. At its peak in 1979 it had about 40 members, and for most of its life it numbered roughly a dozen.

Its best-known project was Kalkul 3, built in 1978. It was an 8-bit machine assembled from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts and was used by the city transport company to check tram timetable schedules. Three units were built. The club was dissolved in 1983 when the culture house withdrew the room. Its papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 board are held by the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno, in fond KM-14.

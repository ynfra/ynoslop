Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole (KVT Královo Pole) was a computer club in Brno, in the Královo Pole district of Czechoslovakia. Founded in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house, the club had about 40 members at its peak in 1979 and roughly a dozen members for most of its life. Its best-known project was Kalkul 3 (1978), an 8-bit machine built from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. Used by the city transport company to check tram timetable schedules, three units were built. The club was dissolved in 1983 when the culture house withdrew the room. Its papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 board are held by the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno, fond KM-14.

# Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole

Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole (KVT Královo Pole) was a computer hobby club in the Královo Pole district of Brno, Czechoslovakia. It was founded in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house.

The club had about 40 members at its peak in 1979, though it had roughly a dozen members for most of its existence.

In 1978, the club built the Kalkul 3, an 8-bit machine made from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. The city transport company used it to check tram timetable schedules. Three units were built.

The club dissolved in 1983 after the culture house withdrew its room. Its papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 board are held by the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno, fond KM-14.

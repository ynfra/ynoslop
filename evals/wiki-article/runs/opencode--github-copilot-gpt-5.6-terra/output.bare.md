**Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole** (KVT Královo Pole) was a hobby section of the local ROH culture house in the Královo Pole district of Brno, Czechoslovakia. Founded in 1974, it had roughly a dozen members for most of its existence and about 40 at its 1979 peak.

In 1978, the club built the 8-bit **Kalkul 3** from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. Three units were made and used by the city transport company to check tram timetable schedules.

The club dissolved in 1983 after the culture house withdrew its room. Its papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 board are held by the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno, fond KM-14.

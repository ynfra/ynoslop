**Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole**

The **Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole** (**KVT Královo Pole**) was a computing hobby club in Brno, Czechoslovakia. Founded in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house, it operated in the Královo Pole district of the city until its dissolution in 1983.

## Membership

The club had roughly a dozen members for most of its existence, reaching a peak of about 40 members in 1979.

## Kalkul 3

The club's notable project was the "Kalkul 3" (1978), an 8-bit machine built from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. It was used by the city transport company to check tram timetable schedules. Three units were built.

## Dissolution and archive

The club was dissolved in 1983, when the culture house withdrew the room. Its club papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 board are held by the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno, fond KM-14.

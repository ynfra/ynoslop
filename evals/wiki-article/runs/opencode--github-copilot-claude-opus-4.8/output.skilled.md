# Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole

**Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole** (KVT Královo Pole) was a computing hobby club in the Královo Pole district of Brno, Czechoslovakia. It was founded in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house.

The club had about 40 members at its peak in 1979, though for most of its existence it numbered roughly a dozen.

Its best-known project was "Kalkul 3" (1978), an 8-bit machine built from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. The city transport company used it to check tram timetable schedules. Three units were built.

The club dissolved in 1983, when the culture house withdrew its room.

The club's papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 board are held by the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno, fond KM-14.

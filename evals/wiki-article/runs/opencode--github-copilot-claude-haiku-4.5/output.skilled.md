# Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole

**Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole** (KVT Královo Pole) was a computer hobbyist club in the Královo Pole district of Brno, Czechoslovakia, founded in 1974 as a section of the local ROH (Revolutionary Trade Union Movement) culture house.

The club had about 40 members at its peak in 1979, though it typically maintained a dozen or so active participants. Its most notable project was "Kalkul 3," an 8-bit computer built in 1978 from surplus Tesla MHB8080 components. Three units were constructed and used by Brno's city transport company to verify tram timetables.

The club dissolved in 1983 after the culture house reclaimed its meeting space. Club records and one surviving Kalkul 3 circuit board are preserved in the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno under fond KM-14.

# Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole

**Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole** (KVT Královo Pole) was a computer hobby club based in the Královo Pole district of Brno, Czechoslovakia.

## History

The club was founded in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house. At its peak in 1979, it had approximately 40 members, though for most of its operational period membership remained at roughly a dozen. The club was dissolved in 1983 when the culture house withdrew its meeting space.

## Notable work

The club's most significant project was the *Kalkul 3* (1978), an 8-bit computer constructed from surplus Tesla MHB8080 components. Three units of the Kalkul 3 were built and used by Brno's city transport company to verify tram timetable schedules.

## Legacy

Club papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 circuit board are preserved in the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno, catalogued under fond KM-14.

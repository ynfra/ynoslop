**Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole** (**KVT Královo Pole**) was a computer hobbyist club in the Královo Pole district of Brno, Czechoslovakia. It was founded in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house. 

The club had roughly a dozen members for most of its existence, though membership reached about 40 at its peak in 1979. 

In 1978, the club built "Kalkul 3," an 8-bit machine constructed from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. The city transport company used this machine to check tram timetable schedules. KVT Královo Pole built three units of the Kalkul 3. 

The club dissolved in 1983 when the culture house withdrew access to its room. 

The Moravian Technical Museum in Brno holds the club's papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 board in fond KM-14.

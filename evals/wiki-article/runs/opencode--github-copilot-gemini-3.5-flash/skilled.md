**Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole** (KVT Královo Pole) was a computer hobby club in Brno, Czechoslovakia. Founded in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house in the Královo Pole district, the group had about 40 members at its peak in 1979. For most of its life, membership remained at roughly a dozen people.

In 1978, the club built "Kalkul 3," an 8-bit machine made from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. The club built three units of the machine, which the city transport company used to check tram timetable schedules.

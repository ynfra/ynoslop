**Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole** (**KVT Královo Pole**) was a computer hobby club based in the Královo Pole district of Brno, Czechoslovakia. Founded in 1974, the club operated until its dissolution in 1983.

## History
The club was founded in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house. For the majority of its existence, the club maintained roughly a dozen members, though membership peaked at about 40 in 1979. KVT Královo Pole was dissolved in 1983 when the culture house withdrew the room used by the club.

## Kalkul 3
In 1978, the club developed its most notable project, "Kalkul 3". This was an 8-bit machine constructed from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. A total of three units were built. The machines were used by the city transport company to check tram timetable schedules.

## Archive
Club papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 board are held by the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno under fond KM-14.

**Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole** (**KVT Královo Pole**) was a computer hobby club based in the Královo Pole district of Brno, Czechoslovakia.

### History
The club was founded in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house. For most of its existence, it had roughly a dozen members, reaching a peak of about 40 members in 1979. KVT Královo Pole was dissolved in 1983 when the culture house withdrew the room it used.

### Kalkul 3
In 1978, the club developed its most notable project, the "Kalkul 3". This was an 8-bit machine constructed from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. Three units were built and used by the city transport company to check tram timetable schedules.

### Archive
The club papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 board are held by the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno under the fond KM-14.

# Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole

**Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole** (KVT Královo Pole) was an amateur
computing club in the Královo Pole district of Brno, Czechoslovakia. It was
founded in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house and dissolved
in 1983.

## Membership

The club had about a dozen members for most of its existence. Membership peaked
at roughly 40 in 1979.

## Kalkul 3

The club's best-known project was Kalkul 3, an 8-bit machine completed in 1978
and assembled from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. The city transport company used
it to check tram timetable schedules. Three units were built.

## Dissolution and archive

The club closed in 1983 after the culture house withdrew the room it had been
using. Its papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 board are held by the Moravian
Technical Museum in Brno as fond KM-14.

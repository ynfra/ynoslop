## Draft

**Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole** (KVT Královo Pole) was founded in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house in the Královo Pole district of Brno, Czechoslovakia. It had roughly twelve members for most of its existence, reaching about 40 members in 1979.

In 1978, the club built the "Kalkul 3", an 8-bit machine using surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. The Brno city transport company used it to check tram timetable schedules. Three units were built.

The club dissolved in 1983 after the culture house withdrew its room. Its papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 board are held by the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno as fond KM-14.

Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole (KVT Královo Pole) was a computing club in Brno, Czechoslovakia, active from 1974 to 1983.

The club began in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house in the Královo Pole district of Brno. Membership stayed small, usually around a dozen people, but reached about 40 at its peak in 1979.

In 1978 the club built "Kalkul 3," an 8-bit machine assembled from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. The city transport company used it to check tram timetable schedules. Three units were built.

The culture house withdrew the club's room in 1983, ending its activity.

Club papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 board are held by the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno, fond KM-14.

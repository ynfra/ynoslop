# Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole

**Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole** (KVT Královo Pole, "Computing Technology Club Královo Pole") was a hobbyist computing club based in the Královo Pole district of Brno, Czechoslovakia.

## History

The club was founded in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH (Revolutionary Trade Union Movement) culture house. Membership peaked at about 40 people in 1979, though for most of the club's existence it comprised roughly a dozen active members.

The club was dissolved in 1983, after the culture house withdrew the room it had been using.

## Kalkul 3

The club's notable project was **Kalkul 3**, completed in 1978. It was an 8-bit machine built from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. Three units were built, and the machine was used by the city transport company to check tram timetable schedules.

## Archive

The club's papers, along with one surviving Kalkul 3 board, are held by the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno, under fond KM-14.

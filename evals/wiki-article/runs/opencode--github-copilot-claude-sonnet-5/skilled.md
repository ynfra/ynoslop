Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole (KVT Královo Pole) was a computing club in the Královo Pole district of Brno, Czechoslovakia. It started in 1974 as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house.

Membership peaked at about 40 in 1979, though for most of its existence the club had roughly a dozen members.

In 1978 the club built "Kalkul 3," an 8-bit machine assembled from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. The city transport company used it to check tram timetable schedules. Three units were built.

The club dissolved in 1983 after the culture house withdrew its room.

Its papers, along with one surviving Kalkul 3 board, are held by the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno, fond KM-14.

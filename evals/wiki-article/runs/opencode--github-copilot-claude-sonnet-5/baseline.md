**Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole**

The Klub výpočetní techniky Královo Pole (KVT Královo Pole, "Computing Technology Club Královo Pole") was a hobbyist computing club founded in 1974 in the Královo Pole district of Brno, Czechoslovakia, as a hobby section of the local ROH culture house.

**Membership**

The club's membership peaked at about 40 in 1979, though for most of its existence it maintained a core of roughly a dozen members.

**Kalkul 3**

The club's most notable project was Kalkul 3 (1978), an 8-bit machine built from surplus Tesla MHB8080 parts. It was used by the city transport company to check tram timetable schedules, and three units were built.

**Dissolution and archive**

The club was dissolved in 1983, when the culture house withdrew the room it had used. Its papers and one surviving Kalkul 3 board are held by the Moravian Technical Museum in Brno, under fond KM-14.
